Siril Auto — Astro Archive

The Python controller and .ssf command generator in this folder are independent
Astro Archive code. They launch the user's separately installed siril-cli.exe.
No Siril executable, library, source module, or third-party automation code is
included. Siril is not a license-free product: its own GPLv3+ terms continue to
apply to Siril. No paid license key is requested by this integration.

logo.svg is the existing application emblem requested by the user: the Siril
icon with Astro Archive/PhotoSorter gears. logo.png is its rasterized copy.
The underlying Siril artwork belongs to its respective authors. Its original
SVG is retained, together with the GPL license supplied by the installed Siril
distribution in Siril-GPLv3.txt. This attribution concerns the reused artwork
and does not claim that it is original Astro Archive artwork or endorsement.
